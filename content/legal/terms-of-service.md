## What Vellon is, and what it is not

Vellon makes software. We license Vellon Dispatch to taxi companies, and they use
it to run their own dispatch operation.

**We do not provide transportation.** We do not operate taxis, we do not own or
maintain vehicles, we do not employ or contract drivers, we do not set fares, and
we do not arrange your ride. When you book through the app, your ride is provided
by a licensed taxi company, and your contract for that ride is with that company —
not with us.

What this means in practice:

- The **taxi company** decides who is dispatched to you, sets the rates, holds the
  municipal licences and permits, insures the vehicles, and is responsible for the
  conduct of its drivers and the ride itself.
- **Vellon** is responsible for the software: your account, the booking screen, the
  dispatch logic that offers the job to a driver, the map, the receipt, and the
  security of the platform.
- If something goes wrong **with the ride** — the driver, the route, the vehicle, an
  item left behind, a fare you dispute — the taxi company is the right first
  contact. Their number is in the app under **Help & Support**.
- If something goes wrong **with the app** — you cannot sign in, a charge looks
  wrong, the map is broken — write to **support@vellon.ca**.

## Booking a ride

When you book, the app sends your request to the taxi company and offers the job
to a driver. A booking is a **request**, not a guarantee: whether a car comes
depends on the company having a driver available, and sometimes there is not one.

- A **fare estimate** shown before you book is an estimate, based on the route and
  the company's rates. The amount you actually pay is calculated at the end of the
  ride.
- For a ride **scheduled in advance**, no driver is assigned when you book. One is
  assigned closer to the pickup time. For a scheduled card ride, the fare is
  confirmed nearer to pickup rather than at booking, so a change in the company's
  rates or in a discount you hold can move it.
- **Estimated arrival times are estimates.** They come from live traffic data and
  they change.
- The company may not be able to serve every address, and some areas are outside
  its service area.

## Fares and payment

**The rates are the taxi company's.** They are configured in the software by the
company, and we do not set them or take a view on them.

**Cash.** You pay the driver directly. We are not part of that payment.

**Card.** You may save a card in the app and pay with it.

- Card details are captured by **Stripe** inside the app and go directly to Stripe.
  We never receive your card number — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
- When you book, a **hold** is placed on your card for the estimated fare. Nothing
  is taken at that point. When the ride ends, we charge the actual fare and the
  hold is released.
- If the ride changes — you move the destination, or the trip is shortened — the
  hold is re-taken for the new amount before the change is applied. If your card
  will not authorise the new amount, the change is refused and the ride continues
  to its original destination.
- **The charge on your statement will show Vellon**, not the taxi company. We
  collect the fare on the company's behalf and account for it to the company —
  or, depending on how that company has chosen to be paid, to the driver —
  keeping only the software fee the company has agreed to pay us. This is a
  billing arrangement between us and the company; it does not make Vellon the
  provider of your ride, and it does not change who you are owed a service by.
- A **receipt** is emailed to you if you have given us an email address.
- **Discounts.** If you hold a verified student discount or a promotional code, it
  is applied automatically at booking. Discounts are the taxi company's, may be
  withdrawn, and cannot be applied after a ride has ended.

If you believe a charge is wrong, write to **support@vellon.ca** and we will look
at it with the taxi company. If the amount charged is the issue, we can correct it;
if the rate itself is the issue, that is the company's decision.

## Cancellations and no-shows

You can cancel a ride yourself, in the app, at any time up until the driver starts
the trip. Once the trip has started, only the taxi company's dispatcher can end it
— contact them.

**The app does not charge a cancellation fee or a no-show fee.** If you cancel, or
if a driver reports that you did not appear, the card hold is released and nothing
is taken. A driver can only report a no-show after waiting at the pickup for
several minutes and while actually at it.

We may cancel a ride the system cannot complete — for example one that has been
waiting a long time with no driver available. If that happens the hold is released.
